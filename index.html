Built like an instrument. Made to keep evolving.

Drummatic is a hand-assembled drum machine in milled and anodized aluminium, with 46 lit keys, 9 encoders and a 5-inch touch screen. It ships with LazerDrum: 64 synthesis engines, a break generator and a 16-pattern sequencer with song mode. Everything inside it can be updated.

The instrument

The knob is next to the value it turns

The screen is laid out like the panel. The eight values on the right sit in two rows of four, exactly like the eight encoders beside them. The bottom of the screen names the fifteen keys above the keyboard, in order. One modifier, FUNC, and one gesture, hold a step to edit it, cover the rest.

A breakbeat machine with a synthesizer behind every key

ENGINES

64 engines, 16 families

Each track plays one of 64 synthesized sounds, and any single step can call a different one. Twelve sound controls per engine: pitch, attack, decay, tone, noise, FM, FM ratio, FM index, resonance, drive, crush and a tail with nine resonant modes.

The 16 families, one by one
GENERATOR

A break generator that knows the genres

Eight styles, from Amen jungle to drill'n'bass, with chop, ghosts, rolls, fills, hats and zaps as live controls. MATRIX builds a whole world in one press: style, tempo, break, kit and all 64 engines.

  • MARKOV: variations learned from your own pattern
  • EVOLVE: the pattern drifts slowly, MANUAL brings it back
  • 28 break presets and 146 sound presets on the DATA encoder, the IDM ones first
SEQUENCER

8 tracks × 64 steps, locks on every step

Hold any key of the sixteen and the encoders write only into that step: velocity, probability, retrig, nudge, pitch, engine, a throw into the echo, a repeat, and a lock on each of the twelve sound controls.

  • Live recording from the eight track keys
  • Copy and paste of pages, tracks and patterns
  • 24 levels of UNDO and REDO
ARRANGE

16 patterns and a song, switched in time

Each project holds 16 patterns sharing one kit. Pick the next one while the current one plays: it waits, blinking, and takes over the moment the current one ends. Chain them into a song of up to 99 rows with repeats, looping or stopping at the end.

EFFECTS

TAPZ, REPEAT, COLOR, mastering

TAPZ is a multi-tap echo with five spatial modes, sync and freeze. REPEAT is a stutter you can hold live or trigger per step. COLOR adds tape, tube, transformer or fuzz. A mastering stage with genre profiles keeps every pattern at a target loudness.

CONNECT

MIDI in four flavours, clock in and out of step

MIDI DIN in and out plus USB MIDI. Incoming notes can play the 64 engines, the 8 tracks, one engine chromatically, or the 16 steps. Set CLOCK to EXT and Drummatic follows start, stop and song position from any machine.

The engines

Sixteen ways to make a sound, four variants each

Every engine is synthesis, not playback. Each picture is the real spectrogram of the four variants of that family, struck one after the other: time runs left to right, pitch rises from bottom to top.

Build quality

Aluminium, steel and switches you can hear

No plastic shell and no glue. The case is machined and bent aluminium held together with countersunk screws and pressed-in steel fasteners, so every part can be opened, serviced and replaced.

MILLED · ANODIZED

Milled side cheeks

CNC-milled from solid aluminium, bead-blasted and anodized black. They give the machine its playing angle.

ANODIZED · ENGRAVED

Engraved top panel

Black anodized aluminium with laser-engraved legends. Everything is fixed from below: nothing shows on the top face but a few screws.

BENT · POWDER-COATED

Bent chassis tray

A single bent sheet with pressed-in steel fasteners, powder-coated matte black, on rubber feet.

MECHANICAL · HOT-SWAP

Mechanical keys, replaceable in seconds

Clicky low-profile switches in hot-swap sockets under transparent caps. Each of the 46 keys has its own RGB LED, so the panel shows tracks, steps and the playhead in colour.

DETENTED · PUSH

Encoders with a push

Nine detented push encoders under solid knobs, and a potentiometer for the volume. A push resets a value or clears a step lock.

PANEL-MOUNT

Panel-mount connectors

Panel-mount USB-C power and USB host, stereo in and out on 6.35 mm jacks, MIDI DIN in and out. Nothing hangs off a circuit board.

Rear panel
Built to evolve

The hardware is finished. The instrument never is.

Drummatic was designed so that what it does can change after you buy it. LazerDrum is where it starts.

Updates

One file on a USB stick

Copy the new firmware to a stick, plug it into USB HOST on the back, and the machine updates itself, including the controller that reads the keys. No computer, no opening the case.

Panel

Neutral by design

Only fixed legends are engraved: numbers, transport, FUNC, arrows, DATA, VOLUME. Every other key is named on the screen, so a new firmware can turn the same panel into a different instrument.

Inside

Standard, serviceable parts

Known, widely available parts with years of headroom, swappable keys, screws instead of glue.

FAQ
Questions

    

      
Do I need a computer?
No. Drummatic is a standalone instrument: power it, play it, plug headphones or a mixer into the outputs. A computer or a phone on the same network can open a virtual copy of the panel in a browser, which is handy but optional.

      
Can it run things other than LazerDrum?
That is the idea behind it. The panel carries only neutral legends and the keys are named on screen, so a different firmware can make it a different instrument. LazerDrum is the first.

      
How are updates installed?
From a USB stick in the USB HOST socket on the back. The same stick updates the key controller, so the case never has to be opened.

      
Can it sync with my other gear?
Yes. It follows MIDI clock with start, stop and song position over DIN or USB, and incoming notes can play its tracks, engines or steps.

      
Is this the final look?
The images are renders of the production design: the case parts, the control board and the keys shown here have been made for the prototype. Small details may still change before the first run.
